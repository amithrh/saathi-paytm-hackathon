# Paytm's merchant stack and AI roadmap as of October 2026 (novelty check for a proactive vernacular voice "CFO agent")

Research date: 31 Oct 2026. Figures are dated inline. "Confirmed" = stated by Paytm or a named outlet; "Inference" = my reading.

## Q1. Does Paytm already proactively call or message merchants about failed settlements, offline Soundboxes or loan eligibility? How are loan offers delivered?

### Takeaway
Paytm's merchant communications today are reactive or push-notification style (SMS/app alerts after a settlement, light-colour codes on the device, a helpline to call). The only "proactive AI call" merchants describe publicly is an automated status-update call about an unresolved ticket, which they find annoying. Loan offers are data-driven (Soundbox/QR history) and surfaced in the Paytm for Business app, with AI used internally for risk and collections; I found no evidence of a voice agent that calls a merchant to explain a settlement failure or an offline device.

### Cited Findings
- Settlement alerts: Paytm sends a "settlement SMS / app notification with reference number and bank account details" after settlement; the support page concedes that "due to network issues you might not receive this notification" and tells merchants to check the Settlements tab, then call the 24x7 helpline 0120-4440440 if the money has not arrived after 2 days — [Paytm for Business support: I didn't receive my settlement](https://business.paytm.com/support/i-didnt-receive-my-settlementpayout)
- Repeated settlement failures: official advice is "usually settled to your bank within T+1 days ... on rare occasions, due to technical issues at the bank's end, some merchants experience settlement failures. If your settlements are getting failed repeatedly, we suggest you to change your linked bank account" — [Paytm for Business support: settlement repeatedly failing](https://business.paytm.com/support/my-settlement-is-repeatedly-failing)
- Offline Soundbox detection is merchant-side, via LED colours: a blinking green light "means the Soundbox is failing to connect to the Paytm network"; fix is remove/reseat SIM and power-cycle; stable blue confirms connection — [Paytm blog: blinking green light fix](https://paytm.com/blog/paytm-help/paytm-soundbox-blinking-green-light-fix/). For "blue light on but not announcing": "restart your device. If the issue persists, please call us at 0120-4440440" — [Paytm support](https://business.paytm.com/support/what-should-i-do-if-the-blue-light-is-on-but-the-device-is-not-repeating-any-transactions). The device announces only successful transactions, never failed/pending ones — [Paytm support](https://business.paytm.com/support/does-sound-box-announce-failedpending-transactions)
- Automated outbound AI calls exist, but only as ticket-status nags: a Play Store reviewer (2026) complained that "every two days, an AI phone call comes in and keeps repeating the exact same thing over and over for an hour: 'We regret that your issue has not been resolved yet'" — [Google Play listing (user review surfaced in search)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.paytm.business)
- Support channel: business.paytm.com advertises "Round-the-clock AI chat support"; no bot name, language list or voice channel is given — [business.paytm.com](https://business.paytm.com/)
- Loan offers are driven by device data: Soundbox gives "invaluable insights into merchant behaviors, facilitating the offering of loans based on this data"; in Q3 FY23 "more than 85% of the value disbursed that quarter was to merchants using a Paytm payment device" (Paytm spokesperson) — [Rest of World, 2023](https://restofworld.org/2023/india-sound-boxes-paytm-phonepe/). Merchant anecdote in same piece: six months after subscribing to Soundbox he "received an instant loan of 3 lakh rupees" and later a second of 5 lakh.
- Loan delivery surface: Paytm for Business app offers "business loan of up to 5 lakh rupees in just 5 minutes ... without any paperwork" (app listing); business.paytm.com now says "Get up to ₹10 lakh Business loan in just 5 minutes" — [mwm.ai app listing](https://mwm.ai/apps/paytm-for-business/1351301966); [business.paytm.com](https://business.paytm.com/)
- Q1 FY27 earnings release (Jul 2026): merchant loan growth is "bolstered by AI-led lifecycle management of device merchants, including AI driven risk insights for partners and collections"; "Loans are underwritten and booked by our lending partners in their balance sheets. Paytm acts as a collection outsourcing partner" — [Paytm Q1 FY27 earnings release PDF](https://paytm.com/document/ir/financial-results/Paytm_Earning-Release_Q1-FY-2027.pdf)
- AGM (Sep 2026): Group CFO Madhur Deora said "all of the company's merchant sales work, including loan collection, is now powered by AI agents" — [Medianama, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-paytm-paytm-intelligence/) / [Inc42, 9 Oct 2026](https://inc42.com/features/payments-vs-lending-the-tale-of-paytms-two-engines/)
- Q1 FY27 call: Sharma said "We have removed the call centre cost" via the in-house AI model — [Medianama, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-paytm-enterprise-ai-service/)
- Lending partners (merchant loans) per the Play Store listing (updated Oct 2026): Clix Capital, SMFG India Credit, Piramal Capital and Housing Finance, Aditya Birla Finance, Credit Saison India, IDFC First Bank, Muthoot Fincorp, Northern Arc Capital, PayU Finance, Profectus Capital, Shriram Finance, Suryoday Bank, Tata Capital, Ugro — [Google Play listing via search](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.paytm.business&hl=en_IN); Feb 2024 count was seven NBFC partners — [Business Standard, Feb 2024](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/paytm-crisis-non-bank-lenders-explore-options-for-loan-disbursal-124021300909_1.html)
- Loan penetration: lending covered "about 7% of its subscription merchant base in Q4 FY26, compared with roughly 6% a year earlier" (Q4 FY26 call) — [Inc42, 9 Oct 2026](https://inc42.com/features/payments-vs-lending-the-tale-of-paytms-two-engines/)
- Collections metrics (June 2026 quarter, merchant loans): Bucket-1 resolution 83-90%, recovery post-90+ 30-35%, ECL 4.5-5.0% — [Paytm Q1 FY27 earnings release PDF](https://paytm.com/document/ir/financial-results/Paytm_Earning-Release_Q1-FY-2027.pdf)

### Inferences
- Paytm's "AI agents" in merchant sales and collections are internal, outbound, Paytm-interest agents (sell devices, collect EMIs). Nothing found positions an agent as the merchant's own advisor that calls them about *their* money (stuck settlement, device offline, cashflow). That gap is where a "CFO agent" is novel for Paytm judges.
- Loan eligibility is almost certainly surfaced as in-app banners/pre-approved offers and sales-team outreach; with 7% penetration of 1.64 Cr subscription merchants, a voice agent that explains an offer in the merchant's language is a plausible lever Paytm would care about (ties to their stated #2 growth engine).
- The "AI phone call every two days" complaint shows Paytm already has outbound robo-voice infrastructure, so judges will not be impressed by "we can make a call"; the novelty has to be in the trigger (proactive detection), the content (merchant-centric diagnosis) and the vernacular quality.

### Gaps
- No public documentation of the exact in-app loan-offer UX (banner, eligibility check, push) or whether Paytm sends outbound pre-approved-offer calls/SMS; Google Play and App Store pages could not be fully fetched.
- No official statement on server-side offline-Soundbox detection or any merchant alert when a device drops off the network (only merchant-facing LED guidance found).
- Paytm stopped disclosing merchant loan disbursement value/count; Q1 and Q2 FY27 releases give only "more than half to repeat borrowers".

## Q2. Does Paytm for Business have khata/udhaar or collection reminders? Offline Soundbox detection?

### Takeaway
Yes on khata: "Paytm Business Khata" (launched c. early 2020) records udhaar, sets due dates and sends automated SMS/WhatsApp reminders to customers. Offline detection is manual (LED colour codes + helpline), per Q1.

### Cited Findings
- Business Khata: "records udhaar, tracks cash & card sales, and collects online payments from customers at zero fees"; features include bill/receipt photos, "SMS & WhatsApp reminders", downloadable reports, adding customers from contacts; merchants can "set payment due dates for credit transactions and send automated reminders" — [business.paytm.com/business-khata](https://business.paytm.com/business-khata)
- Business Khata was launched alongside the All-in-One QR (announced ~Jan 2020) — [The Mobile Indian](https://themobileindian.com/news/paytm-for-business-app-business-khata-and-all-in-one-qr-for-merchant-partners-launched-in-india-29883); [BW Disrupt](https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/paytm-launches-all-in-one-qr-for-merchants-with-unlimited-payments-at-0-fee-181681)
- Business Khata is not featured on the current business.paytm.com homepage (Oct 2026), which lists Soundbox, card machines, business loans, AI Router, reports/insights and AI chat support — [business.paytm.com](https://business.paytm.com/)
- Settlement controls: instant settlement selectable in-app ("tap on your bank account ... select instant settlement") added in release 5.6.8; default cycle T+1/T+2 — [APKMirror release notes](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/paytm-one97-communications-ltd/paytm-for-business-accept-manage-payments/paytm-for-business-accept-manage-payments-5-6-8-release/); [Paytm support T+1](https://business.paytm.com/support/my-settlement-is-repeatedly-failing)
- App insights: Play listing says merchants can "access business insights", receive instant payment alerts, send payment links via WhatsApp/SMS/email; homepage promises "Monthly growth suggestions and customer profiles" — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.paytm.business); [business.paytm.com](https://business.paytm.com/)
- Soundbox offline detection: see Q1 (LED codes, restart, helpline) — [Paytm blog](https://paytm.com/blog/paytm-help/paytm-soundbox-blinking-green-light-fix/)

### Inferences
- Khata is a legacy, text/app-based feature that appears de-emphasised; a voice-first udhaar reminder flow would be an extension, not a first. Pitch it as "voice on top of Khata data" rather than claiming Paytm has no khata.
- Because the Soundbox never announces failed/pending payments and goes silent when offline, a merchant cannot tell "no customers" from "device dead" without looking at LEDs; a proactive "your Soundbox has been offline 40 min, 3 payments landed in-app" call is a real, documented gap.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Business Khata is still actively maintained in the 2026 app (not on homepage; app store pages blocked).
- No public data on how many merchants use Khata.

### Inferences
- Paytm already owns the "talk to your Soundbox in 11 languages for business insights" narrative (pull, on-device, Q&A). The novel angle left is *push*: the agent initiates contact when something is wrong or an opportunity exists, with a CFO-style diagnosis, across merchants who have ordinary (non-AI) Soundboxes. Judges will ask how this differs from AI Soundbox; the answer must be "proactive + works on the 1.6 Cr legacy devices via phone/WhatsApp".
- Paytm's merchant-agent roadmap (Q1 FY27 wording) is marketing/customer-engagement agents (help merchants sell to consumers), not finance/cashflow agents. A CFO agent is adjacent but not announced.
- Using Sarvam models is aligned with the hackathon's sponsor and Paytm's own "fine-tune open-source, low-cost, few tokens" philosophy; it will not read as novel by itself since several entries use Sarvam.
- The Q1 FY27 earnings call quote "We have removed the call centre cost" suggests Paytm support is already heavily automated; a pitch that frames itself as "replace the call centre" is late. Framing as "reduce inbound tickets by catching problems before the merchant notices" fits their cost-to-serve narrative.

### Gaps
- No deployed-unit count, price or merchant reviews for the AI Soundbox as of Oct 2026.
- No named GenAI merchant-support bot, language coverage or voice support channel documented by Paytm.
- Paytm Intelligence: no product page, pricing or customer names; Bloomberg-sourced only.
- Hackathon judging criteria and judges not retrievable (devSemble page returned title only).

## Q5. Public merchant pain points

### Takeaway
Recurring complaints across Play Store, App Store, G2, Voxya and PissedConsumer (2023-2026): unresponsive support and tickets closed unresolved (40-minute holds, cannot raise tickets), Soundbox reliability (missed announcements, defective units, no pickup on deactivation), rental debits continuing after deactivation, settlement holds/stuck funds and settlement mode not matching what was promised, and unexpected fees.

### Cited Findings
- Play Store (Sep 2026): Soundbox "extremely unreliable, with frequent technical issues ... sometimes the payment announcement doesn't work properly"; support "always closed the ticket without resolving it" with holds "more than 40 minutes"; settlement mode: told cumulative/instant were options "but after I received the box only Instant settlement option was enabled by default ... they said after 31 August it will be rectified, but till date there is no update"; ticketing tool "wasn't even allowing them to raise a ticket" — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.paytm.business&hl=en_IN)
- App Store (early 2026): "Worst experience funds stuck no proper support"; Soundbox return problems — [mwm.ai aggregation](https://mwm.ai/apps/paytm-for-business/1351301966)
- G2 (282 reviews): praise for ease of use and daily settlement reports; dislikes: slow support, hidden charges, difficulty cancelling devices, downtime/failed transactions; "settlement timing was cited as the biggest issue" by one reviewer — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/paytm-business/reviews)
- Voxya: "Paytm merchant business amount hold not settle", "Settlement amount is not reflected in Bank", rental debited after deactivation accepted and courier pickup never came — [Voxya](https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/denying-paytm-soundbox-deactivation-request-and-debiting-money-unnecessary-/221058); [Voxya](https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/paytm-soundbox-scam/204613)
- TechnoFino forum: agent promised to collect Soundbox never arrives — [TechnoFino](https://technofino.in/community/threads/paytm-soundbox-problem.21291/)
- Defective unit replacement delays (2022) — [consumercomplaintscourt](https://consumercomplaintscourt.com/paytm-soundbox-is-not-working/)
- PissedConsumer: "This app customer service is bad", pending settlement complaints — [PissedConsumer](https://paytm.pissedconsumer.com/review.html)
- Post-PPBL context: merchants advised to verify the bank account receiving settlements, "not just whether the device announces a payment", and reconcile settlement reports against bank statements — [GizmoTimes](https://www.gizmotimes.com/business/paytm-payments-bank-shutdown/51092); RBI cancelled Paytm Payments Bank's licence 24 Apr 2026 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paytm_Payments_Bank)
- Fraud vector: fake "payment received" sound apps used to scam Soundbox merchants — [PhishGuard](https://www.phishguard.co.in/blog/fake-payment-received-sound-apps-the-soundbox-scam)

### Inferences
- The top three complaint clusters (support loops, silent device failures, settlement confusion) are exactly the events a proactive voice agent would pre-empt; citing these verbatim reviews in the pitch grounds the problem in Paytm's own store listing.
- "Settlement mode mismatch" and "funds stuck" show merchants do not understand settlement states; a CFO agent that narrates "₹X settled, ₹Y pending because of bank holiday, ₹Z on hold" in Hindi addresses a documented confusion rather than a hypothetical one.

### Gaps
- No quantified complaint rates, ticket volumes or NPS; evidence is anecdotal reviews.
- No authoritative report of a mass Soundbox outage in 2025-26.
